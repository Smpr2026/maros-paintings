    The studio

    Greg works from a private studio in Sydney's Sutherland Shire. Most of what leaves the easel starts as someone else's photo: a first car, a restored Falcon, a wave from last summer.

    He paints mostly in black and white oil, then lets a single colour land: a yellow plate, a red badge, a blue barrel. Every piece is signed by hand.
